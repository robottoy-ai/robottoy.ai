Penguin v2 — the layer stack

Seven layers, one concern each, assembled top-down into the open back shell. A failure always belongs to exactly one layer. Outermost first. See the parts in 3D.

L7Skin

The plush suit, zip-off. Fabric and stuffing only — zero electronics, zero function.

Contract: goes on and off without tools; cut it open and you meet smooth plastic.

Test: on/off 20 times; the dressed penguin passes the same tests as the naked one.

L6Sensing

Touch pads on the inner wall, mic behind a pinhole, camera behind its chest window, motion sensor.

Contract: senses through 2.5 mm shell + fur; thresholds set from measured touches (the v1 lesson).

Test: pat detection through shell + fabric; overnight false-trigger soak.

L5Harness

The wiring is a named part: one loom, silicone wire, latching plugs, built on a pegboard from the pin map.

Contract: every joint is a plug; any module swaps with zero soldering; one service loop into the head.

Test: continuity 100%; every plug tugged at 2 kg; the loom photo is the drawing of record.

L4Electronics

Battery (fused, padded, captive in its bay), both brains on standoffs, speaker, camera.

Contract: boards never touch the wall; one power plug per board; bay closes with its own cover.

Test: thermal soak under 45 °C; a stalled motor cannot reboot a brain.

L3Actuation

4 servos, the one-motor waddle drive with 180°-phased cranks, feet, purr motor. Runs on a bare test plate before the shell exists.

Contract: mounts only on numbered points; shafts exit only through bushings; jammed limb slips or cuts off in 2 s.

Test: 10,000 waddle + 10,000 gesture cycles; quieter than v1 at 30 cm.

L2Fastening

Brass heat-set inserts in every boss, M2/M2.5 screws, threadlocker. Installed once, before anything else.

Contract: everything above fastens only into inserts. No glue where a screw can be.

Test: insert survives 5 kg pull; 10 assemble/disassemble cycles.

L1Shell

The two body halves, head shell, foot shells — the only structural part. Bosses, channels, battery bay, windows are features of it.

Contract: a numbered mounting-point map; halves key together only one way.

Test: assembled empty: 70 cm drop, screws cycle 10×, feet stand flat.

Why layers: L3 + L4 start now on the bench (the round-1 parts order). L1 is drawn in parallel against the fit model and frozen when the bench locks dimensions. L5 takes an afternoon once L1 exists; L6 tunes on the real wall; L7 is sewn last. Pattern borrowed from Qoobo's rigid heart under a zip-off suit, and automotive looms that are their own part number.
